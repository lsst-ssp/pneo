# Design records

These are the development records from building pneo's first version
(August–November 2026), kept for maintainers and for readers who want the full
derivation and the reasoning behind individual choices:

| Document | Contents |
|---|---|
| [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) | Goals and scope, the mathematical formalism, architecture, validation plan |
| [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) | Dated log of design decisions (cited elsewhere as D-1, D-2, ...), with rationale and alternatives |
| [IMPLEMENTATION.md](IMPLEMENTATION.md) | The v0 execution plan: work packages (WP0, WP1, ...) and acceptance gates |
| [POPULATION_MODELS.md](POPULATION_MODELS.md) | Survey of solar-system population models available as of 2026-08 |

They are frozen as of 2026-11 and are not kept up to date: some sections
describe plans rather than what was built, and some numbers were superseded by
later validation. The paper (Singh, Jurić & Ivezić, in preparation) is the
authoritative description of the method and its performance. The validation
reports these records cite are in the archived repository lsst-ssp/pneo-full.
